How to build a free client follow-up system

If inquiries arrive through messages, calls, or forms, a simple follow-up process can help you respond consistently. You do not need to pay for software to test one.

Step 1: Give prospects one clear place to inquire

Make a short form with name, contact information, what they need, and permission to contact them. Avoid collecting sensitive information you do not need.

Step 2: Reply promptly

Use this editable template: “Thanks for reaching out about [service]. I received your inquiry. What time works best for a quick conversation?” Only send messages to people who asked to hear from you.

Step 3: Make one helpful follow-up

If someone hasn't responded, send a courteous follow-up in an appropriate channel: “Checking whether you still need help with [service]. Happy to answer any questions.” Honor opt-outs and applicable messaging rules.

Step 4: Track outcomes

Use a free spreadsheet with columns for inquiry date, customer request, next action, and outcome. Review it weekly. Don't automate messages until you know the manual process works.
