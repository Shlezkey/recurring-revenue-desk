## A simple customer inquiry workflow

1. Create a page with one clear action, such as “Request an estimate.”
2. Collect only the details needed to respond.
3. Send a brief acknowledgment that the inquiry was received.
4. Follow up on the specific request and respect communication preferences.
5. Track inquiries, replies, quotes sent, and customers won.

**Copy-and-adapt inquiry acknowledgment:**

> Subject: We received your request  
> Thanks for contacting [Business Name] about [Service]. We've received your inquiry and will review the details. If there's anything else you'd like us to know, reply to this message. — [Name]
