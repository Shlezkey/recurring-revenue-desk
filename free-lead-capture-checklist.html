Free Lead Capture Checklist for Small Businesses

A free checklist to turn website visitors into qualified inquiries. Start with the free methods below before considering paid tools.

  1. Put one clear contact button near the top of every page.
  2. Ask only for name, preferred contact method, and what the visitor needs.
  3. Tell visitors when they should expect a response.
  4. Test the form on a phone and a desktop.
  5. Record each inquiry and the next action in a free spreadsheet.
  6. Review inquiries weekly and improve the most confusing step.
