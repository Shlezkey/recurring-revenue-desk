Need email marketing for multiple websites? Start with this checklist.

If you run several sites and want newsletters, signup forms, and a welcome sequence, don't choose software based on the biggest feature list. Start by checking whether one account can support your actual setup.

Five questions to ask

  1. Multiple sites: Can you connect distinct forms and landing pages to one account, and keep their audiences organized?
  2. Free limits: How many contacts, emails, funnels, and automation steps are included? Check the provider's current plan details.
  3. Consent: Can each form capture the subscriber's permission and provide an unsubscribe mechanism?
  4. Welcome flow: Can you send a confirmation and a short sequence tailored to the specific website?
  5. Upgrade path: What will change in price if your list or sending volume grows?

A simple starting workflow

Create one signup form for each website, label subscribers by their source, send a relevant welcome email, and measure which subscribers engage. Don't combine unrelated lists without the appropriate permission.
