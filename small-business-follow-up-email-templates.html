Five Helpful Customer Follow-Up Email Templates

Five respectful follow-up templates for small business inquiries. Start with the free methods below before considering paid tools.

  1. New inquiry: Thanks for reaching out about [service]. What would you like to know first?
  2. After a call: Thanks for speaking today. Here is a recap of [next step].
  3. Quote follow-up: Checking whether you had any questions about the quote I sent.
  4. Scheduling: I have availability [times]. Would either work for you?
  5. Closing the loop: I haven't heard back, so I'll close this inquiry for now. You're welcome to reach out anytime.
