Set up a free lead capture funnel for your service business

If you're a cleaner, contractor, mobile technician, or other service provider, you can start collecting and following up with inquiries before investing in a full CRM.

Step 1: Make one clear offer

Instead of a generic “contact us” page, try “Request a free estimate” or “Ask about availability.” Say what happens after someone submits the form.

Step 2: Ask only what you need

Start with name, preferred contact method, service requested, and an optional short description. Explain how you'll use their details. Avoid collecting sensitive information unnecessarily.

Step 3: Confirm receipt

Send a brief confirmation that the inquiry arrived. Example: “Thanks for reaching out. We received your request and will review it shortly.” Don't promise a response time you can't meet.

Step 4: Follow up thoughtfully

If the prospect has opted into appropriate messages, send one relevant follow-up. Respect opt-outs and applicable email and privacy requirements. For transactional inquiries, keep the response related to the request.

Step 5: Track results

Count inquiries received, replies, estimates sent, and customers won. Only add automation if it saves time or improves the customer experience.
