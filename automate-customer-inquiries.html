What to automate first when customer inquiries pile up

Begin with low-risk automations that improve response time.

  1. Start with an instant receipt confirmation so the customer knows the form worked.
  2. Route inquiries to a monitored inbox or shared task list.
  3. Add reminders for staff when a request has not been answered.
  4. Avoid sending complex quotes or promises automatically without checking accuracy.
  5. Test exceptions: duplicates, incomplete forms, and customers who ask not to be contacted.

Automation should support human service, not replace judgment.
