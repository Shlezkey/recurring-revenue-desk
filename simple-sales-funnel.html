How to build a simple sales funnel without a big budget

A straightforward funnel for a service business.

  1. Choose one specific service and one ideal customer.
  2. Create a landing page with a concise benefit, relevant proof, and one clear action.
  3. Use a short inquiry form and a clear confirmation message.
  4. Respond to inquiries promptly and qualify them before pitching.
  5. Review where visitors stop progressing, then improve one step at a time.

A funnel is simply a repeatable path from interest to an informed decision.
