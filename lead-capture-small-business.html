How to capture leads on a small business website

A practical way to collect inquiries without overwhelming visitors.

  1. Put one clear call to action near the top of each important page.
  2. Ask only for information needed to answer the inquiry, such as name, email, and the requested service.
  3. Tell visitors what happens after they submit the form, and link to your privacy information.
  4. Test the form on a phone and verify that messages arrive where your team checks them.
  5. Track qualified inquiries rather than celebrating raw form submissions.

A lead-capture page works best when it answers one question: what should the visitor do next?
