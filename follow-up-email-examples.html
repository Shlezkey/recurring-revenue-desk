Three helpful follow-up emails for service businesses

Simple examples for contacts who asked to hear back.

  1. Acknowledgment: “Thanks for reaching out. We received your request about [service] and will respond by [time].”
  2. Clarification: “To recommend the right next step, could you share [one relevant detail]?”
  3. Permission-based check-in: “Following up on your request. Would you still like help with [service]? If not, no action is needed.”
  4. Only send marketing follow-ups where you have appropriate consent; honor unsubscribe requests and applicable rules.

A useful follow-up provides clarity, not pressure.
