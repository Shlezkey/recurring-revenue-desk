How to stop losing leads after the first inquiry

When a prospective customer asks for a quote, the first response matters. A simple process can help you stay organized without sending pushy messages.

1. Capture the essentials

Use a short form asking for name, preferred contact method, and what help they need. Explain how their information will be used.

2. Reply promptly

Send a helpful acknowledgment, then personally answer the question or provide the next step.

3. Follow up thoughtfully

For contacts who agreed to receive follow-ups, schedule one helpful reminder. Include a clear way to stop future messages.

4. Measure what works

Track inquiries, qualified leads, and customers. Don't confuse clicks with revenue.
